Northern Hemisphere Tech & Consulting Inc. has been operating in Canada since 2012.

Over that time, the company has provided technology consulting and services for organizations including WestJet, Shaw Communications—now part of Rogers—ENMAX, and American Express.

That experience across aviation, communications, energy, and financial services informs a practical way of working: understand the operating environment, make technology choices with care, and stay accountable through delivery.
